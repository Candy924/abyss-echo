Privacy Policy

Abyss Echo · Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Summary

The Game itself collects no personal data and sends no gameplay data to us. We run no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The only data stored is your game progress, kept locally in your browser or app storage on your device.

2. What is stored on your device

ItemWherePurpose
Game progress (upgrades, gear, unlocks, settings)localStorageSo your progress survives closing the game
Language and volume preferenceslocalStorageRemember your settings

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing browser/site data, or by uninstalling the app. You can also export a backup from Settings → Export save.

3. What we never collect

  • Name, email, phone number, address
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Account credentials (the game has no login)
  • Gameplay telemetry sent to us

4. Third-party services

Depending on where you play, the following third parties may process limited technical data:

ServiceWhenWhat may be processed
Hosting (GitHub Pages)Loading the web versionIP address and user agent in server logs
CrazyGames / Poki SDKPlaying on those platformsDevice and advertising identifiers, ad interactions — per their own policies
Ad network (if used)Watching a rewarded adAd delivery and measurement data
Store platform (TapTap / Steam)Installing or launchingHandled by that platform under its own policy

We do not control these providers. Please review their privacy policies.

5. Ads

Some content is unlocked by watching a rewarded ad. Ads are provided by the platform or by an ad network it uses. Where required by law, the platform will ask for consent before serving personalised ads. You can simply choose not to watch and keep playing without that reward.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected by a third-party ad provider, contact us and we will help you reach that provider.

7. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on our side. For data processed by the platform or ad providers, exercise your rights directly with them. We will assist where we reasonably can.

8. Security

Progress data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, so there is no server-side store to breach. Keep backups if the progress matters to you (Settings → Export save).

9. Changes

If a future version introduces online features (for example cloud saves), this policy will be updated first and announced in the in-game news panel.

10. Contact

隱私權政策

深淵迴響 · 最後更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

1. 摘要

本遊戲本身不收集任何個人資料，也不會把遊玩資料傳送給我們。我們沒有伺服器、沒有帳號系統、沒有分析工具。唯一的資料是你的遊戲進度，儲存在你裝置上的瀏覽器或 App 儲存空間。

2. 裝置上儲存了什麼

項目位置用途
遊戲進度（強化、裝備、解鎖、設定）localStorage關閉遊戲後仍保留進度
語言與音量偏好localStorage記住你的設定

你可隨時清除瀏覽器／網站資料或解除安裝 App 來刪除；也可在「設置 → 匯出存檔」自行備份。

3. 我們絕不收集

  • 姓名、電郵、電話、地址
  • 精確位置、通訊錄、相片、鏡頭、麥克風
  • 帳號密碼（本遊戲沒有登入）
  • 傳送給我們的遊玩行為資料

4. 第三方服務

依你遊玩的平台不同，以下第三方可能處理有限技術資料：

服務時機可能處理的資料
靜態主機（GitHub Pages）載入網頁版伺服器日誌中的 IP 與 User-Agent
CrazyGames／Poki SDK在該平台上遊玩裝置與廣告識別碼、廣告互動（依其政策）
廣告聯播網（若有）觀看獎勵廣告廣告投放與成效數據
商店平台（TapTap／Steam）安裝或啟動由該平台依其政策處理

我們無法控制上述服務，請參閱其隱私權政策。

5. 廣告

部分內容需觀看獎勵廣告解鎖。廣告由平台或其使用的廣告聯播網提供；法律要求時，平台會先取得你的同意才投放個人化廣告。你也可以選擇不看廣告、不領取該獎勵繼續遊玩。

6. 兒童

本遊戲並非針對 13 歲以下兒童。我們不會刻意收集兒童資料。若你認為第三方廣告商收集了兒童資料，請聯絡我們，我們會協助你向該廠商反映。

7. 你的權利（GDPR／CCPA）

由於我們不持有你的個人資料，我們這端沒有可供匯出、更正或刪除的資料。若涉及平台或廣告商處理的資料，請直接向他們行使權利；我們會在合理範圍內協助。

8. 安全

進度資料只存在你的裝置，不會傳送給我們，因此沒有伺服器端資料庫可供入侵。若進度對你重要，請自行備份（設置 → 匯出存檔）。

9. 變更

若未來版本加入需要網路的功能（例如雲端存檔），本政策會先更新並於遊戲內公告欄公布。
