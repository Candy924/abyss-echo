## Full description

ABYSS ECHO

A fast, one-thumb roguelike. Every run generates new rooms, new monsters, new loot.

◆ 20 chapters, 15 hand-picked real-world scenes
From catacombs and floating cloud isles to neon cities, lava depths, cathedrals and the final throne room. Each chapter has its own monster family — 60 named enemies, not recolors.

◆ 16 playable heroes
Knight, mage, ranger, samurai, paladin, assassin… plus three premium heroes:
Astromancer (8 meteors across the arena), Chronomancer (freeze time for 4s), Dragonkin (transform and breathe destruction).

◆ Echo Rewind — the signature mechanic
Death is not the end. Rewind 3 seconds and try again, once per run. Complete with a giant clock turning backwards.

◆ Nuclear strike
Unlocked after forging every legendary and mythic weapon. A 5-second air-raid siren, then the bomb lands: everything on screen dies instantly.

◆ 72 weapons, 6 rarities
Pistols, shotguns, crossbows, rocket launchers, lasers, staves, greatswords, chains — each with its own projectiles and sound.
10 legendary / mythic equipment pieces, each with a unique passive.

◆ Permanent progression
Gems persist after death. Spend them on 12 permanent upgrades and 16 hero unlocks.

◆ Built for phones
Virtual stick, auto-aim, touch skill buttons. Portrait or landscape.

◆ No ads. No energy. No gacha.
One HK$1 unlock pack gets you all premium content.
